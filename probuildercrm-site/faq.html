Questions builders actually ask us

Everything you need to know before booking a demo.

Pro Builder CRM is a CRM built specifically for real estate builders and developers. It manages projects, unit inventory, customer bookings and payments, bank loan disbursements, invoices and quotations, contractor and broker ledgers, and investor accounts — all in one system, instead of scattered spreadsheets and WhatsApp chats.
Independent real estate builders and developers, construction contractors selling units, and multi-branch real estate companies with a team of supervisors or sales staff. It's used by builders in India, the United States, United Kingdom, Australia and Canada.
General-purpose CRMs are built around leads and deals, not a physical unit's booking status, installment schedule, bank loan disbursements and possession commitments. Pro Builder CRM is modeled around exactly that real estate workflow from day one, so there's no lengthy customization needed to make it fit a builder's business.
Most builders are recording real payments within a day. Add your projects and units, bring in your existing customers and bookings, and you're live — no lengthy implementation project required.
