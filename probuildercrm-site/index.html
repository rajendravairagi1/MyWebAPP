The Problem

Excel, WhatsApp and memory aren't a system.

Most builders run their whole business across scattered Excel sheets, a diary for site notes, and hundreds of WhatsApp chats. One missed installment, one lost paper, one confused broker commission — and it costs real money and a customer's trust.

Scattered records

Customer payments in one sheet, bank loan status in another, site photos in a phone gallery no one can find later.

Missed follow-ups

An installment due date slips, a hot lead goes cold, a possession commitment is forgotten — until the customer calls angry.

No real numbers

You can't answer "how much have we actually collected across all projects this month?" without a day of Excel work.

The Solution

Everything a builder needs, built specifically for how builders work

Not a generic sales CRM adapted for real estate — Pro Builder CRM was designed from day one around projects, units, bookings, installments and possession.

Projects & Unit Inventory

Track every project and every unit's status — available, booked or sold — with photos, floor plans and papers attached, right down to sqft and price.

Customers & Payments

Every customer's full payment history in one place — installments, dues, receipts, and which account each payment landed in.

Bank Loans & Disbursements

Track sanctioned amounts, disbursements, and which came from the bank vs. direct from the customer — auto-reconciled into the same ledger.

Invoices, Quotations & Statements

Auto-generated receipts on every payment, professional quotations and invoices, and a full downloadable customer statement — done for you.

Available Properties & Sharing

A shareable link and PDF for every property, ready to send on WhatsApp — with photos, price and your contact details, no design work needed.

Contractors, Brokers & Investors

Track every payment to labor contractors and vendors, broker commissions, and investor returns — with statements ready to hand over any time.
