Features

One CRM, every part of your real estate business

From the first site visit to the final possession handover — here's everything Pro Builder CRM handles for you.

Projects & Inventory

Every project, every unit, always up to date

Create a project once, then manage every unit inside it — type, area, price, status. See at a glance what's available, booked or sold, with photos, floor layouts and legal papers attached to each one.

  • Unit-wise status: available, booked, sold
  • Photos, floor plans and papers with download
  • Private notes on any unit (e.g. plans to subdivide)
  • Project-wise cost tracking: land, construction, material, labor

Customers & Payments

Never lose track of who owes what

Every customer's full history — every installment, every receipt, which bank account it landed in — in one clean timeline. Record a payment once and the customer's balance, the project's totals, and their auto-generated receipt invoice all update instantly.

  • One-tap "Record Payment" with double-entry protection
  • Auto-generated receipt invoice for every payment
  • Downloadable, shareable customer statement (PDF)
  • Follow-ups, meetings and commitment-date reminders
Bank Loans

Loan disbursements, without the reconciliation headache

Record the sanctioned amount and every disbursement against a customer's unit — and mark whether a payment came from the customer directly or from their bank loan. Everything still rolls into one accurate "Collected" total, and a bank-ready loan statement is one click away.

  • Direct-payment vs. bank-loan toggle on every entry
  • Sanctioned amount, interest rate and disbursement tracking
  • Bank-ready loan statement PDF, per customer

Invoices & Quotations

Professional paperwork, without the paperwork

Turn a quotation into an invoice in one click. Every receipt is generated automatically the moment a payment is recorded — no one has to remember to raise it. Products & services, tax, discounts, all handled.

  • Quotation → Invoice conversion in one click
  • Auto-numbered, sequential, collision-safe invoice numbers
  • Branded PDF output, ready to email or WhatsApp
Available Properties & Sharing

Turn every listing into a shareable brochure

Generate a public, no-login link for any available property — complete with photos, price and your contact details — and share it on WhatsApp in one tap. A downloadable PDF brochure is ready too, so you never have to design a flyer again.

  • One-click shareable property link + PDF brochure
  • A public "business card" profile page for you, listing every open property
  • Direct resale/property deals tracked alongside your own projects

Built for the whole team, not just you

Contractors & Vendors

One place for every labor contractor, vendor and trade — full payment history and a statement to hand them, across all your projects.

Brokers & Commissions

Track commission earned, paid, and the balance owed to every broker — with a statement and a billing invoice ready any time.

Investors

Investment, profit credited, and payouts — a clean ledger you can hand any investor as proof of account.

Team & Permissions
