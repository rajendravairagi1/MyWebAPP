HazriBook – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

HazriBook ("the app") is a record-keeping app for labour contractor agencies. It helps you keep labour attendance, payments, company contracts and invoices. This policy explains what happens to your information.

1. Your data stays on your phone

All information you enter (labour names, mobile numbers, addresses, photos, documents, attendance, payments, company and invoice details, your business profile) is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. The app does not send this information to us or to any server.

2. What we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information from the app. The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools, and it does not need an internet connection to work.

3. Permissions the app may use

  • Camera / photo picker – only when you choose to take or select a picture (for a labourer's photo, a document such as an ID card, a contract copy, or your business logo / QR image). The picture is copied into the app's private storage on your device.
  • Sharing – when you tap share on a PDF statement, invoice, report or backup file, your phone's share sheet opens and the file goes only to the app and person you choose. We do not see it.

4. Sensitive information you store

You may store identity documents and personal details of your workers. You are responsible for having their permission to record this information and for keeping your phone and backup files safe. We recommend setting a screen lock on your phone and keeping backup files private.

5. Backups

The "Backup & export" feature creates a file containing all your data and lets you send it where you choose. Anyone who receives that file can read it, so share it carefully.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete a record inside the app where deletion is allowed. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from phone settings, permanently removes all data held by the app on that device. Because we do not hold a copy, we cannot restore it.

7. Children

The app is for business use by adults and is not directed at children.

8. Future changes

If we add features that send data off your device (for example cloud backup or sync), we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any such data leaves your device.
