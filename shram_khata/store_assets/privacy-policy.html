HazriBook – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

HazriBook ("the app") is a record-keeping app for labour contractor agencies. It helps you keep labour attendance, payments, company contracts and invoices. This policy explains what happens to your information.

1. Your data is on your phone first

All information you enter (labour names, mobile numbers, addresses, photos, documents, attendance, payments, company and invoice details, your business profile) is stored on your device, inside the app's private storage. The app works without an internet connection.

2. Optional cloud sync

If you choose Cloud sync (More → Cloud sync) and create a cloud account, the records you enter (workers, attendance, payments, companies, contracts, invoices, business profile, and the team logins you create) are copied to our server (hazribook.oneweblink.com) so that you can use the app on more than one phone and keep a safe copy. Your account password is stored only as a salted hash. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Photos and document images stay on the phone where they were added and are not uploaded.

  • Who can see it: only the people you give a login to. Team members (supervisors, accountants) see what their role allows. A company manager login sees only that one company: its sites, the workers who work there, their attendance and the bills issued to that company. Managers never see what you pay your workers, advances or payment records.
  • Messages: a company manager can write messages to the agency. These are stored on the server and shown to the owner and supervisors.
  • Our use: we use your data only to run the app for you. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for analytics.
  • Deleting it: the owner can delete the whole cloud account and all data stored online at any time from More → Cloud sync → "Delete my cloud account and data". You can also email us to request deletion. Data on your phones is not affected.

If you never turn on Cloud sync, nothing leaves your device and we receive no information from the app.

2a. What we do not collect

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking tools.

3. Permissions the app may use

  • Camera / photo picker – only when you choose to take or select a picture (for a labourer's photo, a document such as an ID card, a contract copy, or your business logo / QR image). The picture is copied into the app's private storage on your device.
  • Sharing – when you tap share on a PDF statement, invoice, report or backup file, your phone's share sheet opens and the file goes only to the app and person you choose. We do not see it.

4. Sensitive information you store

You may store identity documents and personal details of your workers. You are responsible for having their permission to record this information and for keeping your phone and backup files safe. We recommend setting a screen lock on your phone and keeping backup files private.

5. Backups

The "Backup & export" feature creates a file containing all your data and lets you send it where you choose. Anyone who receives that file can read it, so share it carefully.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete a record inside the app where deletion is allowed. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from phone settings, permanently removes all data held by the app on that device. If you use Cloud sync, delete the cloud account first (see section 2) to also erase the online copy. If you do not use Cloud sync we hold no copy and cannot restore your data.

7. Children

The app is for business use by adults and is not directed at children.

8. Future changes

If we change how data is handled we will update this policy and the date above. Features that send data off your device are always switched off until you turn them on.
