## App name (max 30)
Shram Khata: Labour Manager

## Short description (max 80)
Labour attendance, daily payments and company billing for contractor agencies.

## Full description (max 4000)
Run your labour contractor agency from one simple app. Shram Khata keeps attendance, daily payments, salary balances and company bills in one place, and works offline on site.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Attendance in one tap
- Supervisors mark Present, Half day or Absent for every labourer, with overtime hours.
- Works without internet. A submitted sheet is locked so entries cannot be changed by mistake.
- A mechanic or engineer who visits several companies in a day can be marked at each site.

Payments and salary ledger
- Record daily payments, advances, settlements and fines, by cash, UPI or bank.
- See each worker's earnings, payments and balance at any time, with a "who to pay" list.
- Payments are never deleted, only voided with a reason, so your records stay trustworthy.

Companies, contracts and bills
- Add companies, their sites and contracts with billing rates for each skill.
- Create numbered invoices with optional GST, receipts and outstanding balance.
- Every invoice carries an attendance sheet so the company can verify each day billed.

PDF statements for everyone
- Labour statement with a P / H / A calendar, earnings, payments and balance.
- Company invoice, attendance register, payment register and balance reports.
- Your logo, name, tax ID, address, phone, website, email and payment QR on every page. Hindi names print correctly.

Dashboard
- Total labour, present, absent and half day for the day.
- What you earned, what you spent on labour, what you paid out, and your margin.
- Sites that have not submitted attendance, overdue invoices, expiring documents and contracts.

Team and roles
- Solo, Owner + Team, or Company plans for several branches.
- Add supervisors and accountants, create custom roles, and limit supervisors to their own sites.

Your data stays private on your phone. There are no ads.

Made for contractors who supply workers to factories, plants and project sites.
